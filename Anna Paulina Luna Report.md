## Executive Summary

Anna Paulina Luna is the U.S. Representative for Florida's 13th congressional district. On the surface, she fits a familiar political archetype: a young, photogenic conservative who rose from modest beginnings through military service, media work, and grassroots activism to win a seat in Congress. But a forensic examination of the public record reveals a more complicated picture — one of systematic identity reconstruction, unusual financial concentration, unprecedented foreign contacts, and a pattern of using legal and legislative mechanisms to serve multiple, sometimes contradictory, interests.

Here is what the evidence shows, in plain terms:

### The Money

Luna has concentrated roughly 85% of her disclosed personal wealth — up to $500,000 — into a single private oil-and-gas investment vehicle called **America First Natural Resources LLC**. That company was founded by her largest political donor, **Bruce Rosenthal**, an investment banker who has given her campaigns more than $40,000 and has given over $330,000 total to hard-right candidates. Luna sits on the **House Natural Resources Committee**, which oversees energy policy on federal lands. She voted YEA on H.R. 1, a bill that expanded oil and gas drilling on federal lands, decreased offshore royalty rates, ended methane emission royalties, and directed lease sales in the Arctic National Wildlife Refuge — all directly benefiting domestic oil and gas producers like AFNR.

She also championed a stock-trading ban for Congress (H.R. 1908) that contains a "small business concern" exemption her own investment likely slips through. Her office has confirmed she owns **no publicly traded stocks** — meaning her AFNR holding is likely a debt instrument or partnership interest entirely outside the bill's scope.

Her husband, **Andrew Gamberzky**, is employed by **AEVEX Aerospace**, a defense contractor that was awarded a **$47,973,846 Army contract** in August 2024 and a **$15.6 million Air Force contract**. AEVEX is sanctioned by the Chinese government for Taiwan arms sales and has a Memorandum of Understanding with Taiwan's aerospace industry. Luna sits on the **Foreign Affairs Committee** and the **Oversight & Government Reform Committee**. No recusal or disclosure of potential conflict has been documented.

### Foreign Influence — Russia

Luna has engaged in sustained, direct contact with Russian government officials that is unprecedented for a junior member of Congress:

- She **accepted gifts** (flowers and chocolates bearing Vladimir Putin quotes) from **Kirill Dmitriev**, a sanctioned Putin envoy who runs a fund the U.S. Treasury calls a "slush fund for Putin." Dmitriev is a Specially Designated National (SDN) under U.S. sanctions.
- She **hosted five sanctioned Russian lawmakers** inside the U.S. Capitol in March 2026, giving them a private tour. They entered the Speaker's suite unannounced. One of them, **Alexei Chepa**, has reported intelligence officer ties and a documented partnership with convicted arms dealer Arcadi Gaydamak.
- She **received a 350-page Russian intelligence dossier** on the JFK assassination from Russian Ambassador Alexander Darchiev — the first time in history Congress has received these files. The dossier contained an embedded propaganda proposal for a "Kennedy-Khrushchev World Peace Bridge" over the Bering Strait.
- Her staff internally described Ukraine aid as "warmongering" and claimed JFK was assassinated because he pursued peace with Russia.
- She **co-sponsored a bill to withdraw the U.S. from NATO** (H.R. 6508) — a long-standing Russian strategic objective.
- She **served as a critical node in a Russian disinformation operation** about Ukrainian President Zelenskyy, amplifying a false $50 million claim that Russian state media then cited back as proof.

### Foreign Influence — Israel

Luna's pro-Israel stance appears to have been **manufactured for electoral purposes**. She had no documented Israel-related statements before November 2, 2022 — six days before the election — when she first claimed Messianic Jewish heritage to deflect criticism about her alliance with Marjorie Taylor Greene and to compete with Jewish Democrat Eric Lynn.

The Washington Post later found **no Ashkenazi ancestry**, confirmed her paternal grandfather was a **Nazi Wehrmacht soldier**, and documented that her father was Catholic, not Jewish. Born **Anna Paulina Mayerhofer** ("White, not of Hispanic origin" on her 2015 Florida voter registration), she became **Anna Paulina Luna** ("Hispanic") in 2019, the same year she launched her political career. She has used stalking injunctions against both a political opponent and her own uncle — the same uncle who later exposed her grandfather's Nazi service to the press.
