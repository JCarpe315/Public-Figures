## The Brutal Truth

**No one currently in Congress has both the incentive and the institutional mechanism to act on this report.**

The Senate Ethics Committee is **designed to protect senators, not investigate them.** It requires a senator to initiate a complaint, and no senator — Democrat or Republican — benefits from doing so.

The DOJ will not act because:
- The Logan Act is a dead letter
- FARA requires proof of foreign direction that would need subpoenaed financial records
- The STOCK Act penalty is a $200 civil fine

1. **A whistleblower** with documents goes to the FBI or DOJ directly

3. **House Democrats win the majority** and use Oversight Committee subpoenas to investigate
4. **Fetterman switches parties** — which would trigger Democrats to finally attack him as a traitor, though by then the damage is done
