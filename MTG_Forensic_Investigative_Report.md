## 5. THE MONEY: FINANCIAL FLOW ANALYSIS

### 5.1 The Low-Income Housing Tax Credit (LIHTC) Revenue Stream

TCI performed substantial work on LIHTC-financed affordable housing projects. The money flow operates as follows:

1. **IRS Allocation:** Tax credits allocated to state housing finance agencies by population formula.
2. **State Award:** Credits competitively awarded to developers via Qualified Allocation Plans.
3. **Equity Sale:** Developers sell tax credits to private investors (banks, corporations) for cash equity covering 40–70%+ of costs.
4. **Construction Budget:** Remaining financing from tax-exempt bonds, conventional debt, HUD programs (HOME, CDBG), soft loans.
5. **Contractor Payment:** TCI, as general contractor, is paid from the construction budget.

**Who Pays:** Ultimately, U.S. taxpayers pay through: (a) foregone federal tax revenue from LIHTC allocations, (b) HUD program funds, (c) state and local bond issuances, and (d) indirect subsidies. Private investors receive tax benefits; developers receive profits; contractors like TCI receive direct payments.

**MTG's Take:** As 51% owner, she received $1M–$5M annually in ownership distributions from a company whose revenue was substantially derived from taxpayer-subsidized projects she publicly opposed.
