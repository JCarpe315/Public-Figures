## Part 1: Who Is Candace Owens?

### The Basics
Candace Amber Owens was born on April 29, 1989, in White Plains, New York. Her parents divorced when she was around 11 or 12, and she was raised primarily by her grandparents in Stamford, Connecticut. Her grandfather, Robert Owens Sr., was African American, born in North Carolina. Her grandmother was from Saint Thomas, U.S. Virgin Islands. That Caribbean-American heritage is part of her identity, though she rarely discusses it.

She went to Stamford High School, then attended the University of Rhode Island for three years as a journalism major. Here's the first anomaly: **some sources claim she graduated with a bachelor's degree or even a double bachelor's. Authoritative sources — Wikipedia, Britannica, EBSCO — confirm she left URI after three years with no degree, citing student loan issues.** [HIGH]

**Additional anomaly:** Britannica lists her birth date as April 29, **1969** — off by 20 years. [HIGH — documented error]

After college, she interned at Vogue magazine in New York. Then she worked at an unnamed private equity firm, rising from administrative assistant to "VP of administration." The name of that firm has never been disclosed. [LOW — single source, unnamed]

### The Pivot
In 2015, Owens started a blog called Degree180. The content would shock her current fans. She wrote anti-Trump, anti-GOP, pro-Democrat articles with headlines like:
- "Good luck with #Trump2016" (May 2015)
- "Donald Trump: The 'Perfect' Candidate for the GOP's 'Racist' Base" (January 2016)
- "Why I'm a Democrat" (February 2016)
- "I don't like the GOP" (March 2016)

These articles are archived. They exist. [HIGH]

In 2016, she launched SocialAutopsy.com — an anti-bullying platform that would dox people who posted racist content online. Owens then fell deep into Gamergate conspiracy theories, accusing Quinn and others of faking harassment. [HIGH — archived articles, Kickstarter records]

Sometime in 2017, she flipped. She deleted the anti-Trump content, rebranded as "Red Pill Black," and began posting pro-Trump videos. In November 2017, Charlie Kirk hired her as Director of Urban Engagement at Turning Point USA (TPUSA). By 2018, she was a conservative star.

### THE VON HOLTEN NAME — HISTORICAL COLONIAL CONNECTION

The name "von Holten" (also "van Holten") is not merely a Dutch/Danish surname — it is deeply embedded in the colonial history of the Danish West Indies (now the U.S. Virgin Islands):

- **Joachim Melchior von Holten** (1671–1708): Governor of the Danish West Indies from 1706 to 1708. He came to St. Thomas in 1690 as a bookkeeper for the Danish West India Company, became chief bookkeeper in 1692, and was appointed governor in 1706. He married Maria van Beverhoudt, daughter of a local planter, in 1692. [HIGH — Wikipedia]

- **Christian Ludvig von Holten** (1774–1829): Danish military officer and colonial administrator who served as Governor of St. Thomas and St. John from 1815 to 1819 and again in 1820. He was succeeded by Peter von Scholten. He owned the plantation Frydendal around 1816. [HIGH — Wikipedia]

- The Danish West India Company established control over St. Thomas in 1672 and controlled the island until 1754. The U.S. acquired the Danish West Indies (St. Thomas, St. John, St. Croix) from Denmark on March 31, 1917.

**What this means:** Candace Owens' paternal grandmother (Nola VanHolten) was born in Charlotte Amalie, St. Thomas, in 1942 — 25 years before the U.S. acquired the islands from Denmark. Her family name (VanHolten/Von Holten) appears in Danish colonial records dating back to the 1690s. Whether this indicates actual descent from the colonial von Holten family or is simply a coincidental shared surname among the Afro-Caribbean population of St. Thomas cannot be determined from open sources. However, the historical resonance is significant. [HIGH — Wikipedia, historical records]
